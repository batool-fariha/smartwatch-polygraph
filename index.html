03 How it works

One number, four parts

Each check becomes a score out of 100. The four are weighted into the one number you see.

Sleep
30%

Each night is checked against the heart rate and steps inside it, for duplicate sources, for sleeps over 12 hours and for missing pulse.

Steps
25%

The raw step total against the same minutes counted once. The more the export overstates, the lower the score.

Coverage
25%

How much of each day has watch data, measured against an 18-hour day.

Heart rate
20%

Readings outside 30–230 bpm, and single-reading spikes that happen outside a workout.

Weights and thresholds are hand-picked. They haven’t been validated against a clinical reference.

04 Limits

What this can’t tell you

A short list of what this check can’t see, so the score doesn’t get over-read.

Contradictions, not errors

Two signals disagreeing means something is off. It doesn’t say which one, or whether the sensor is accurate.

Apple Health only, for now

Fitbit and Garmin exports are laid out differently, and Garmin’s doesn’t include minute-level data.

Not a medical device

It checks data quality. It doesn’t diagnose, monitor or advise on anything.
